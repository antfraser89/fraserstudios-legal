NEARLY! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

NEARLY! is a precision game developed by Fraser Studios, operated by Anthony Fraser in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how information is handled in the current app, including the TestFlight beta.

Progress on your device

The app saves game scores, personal bests, recent results and statistics, XP and achievements, Daily history and streaks, settings and tutorial preferences on your device. It also stores randomly generated player and attempt identifiers, timestamps and completed-ad retry records to remember progress and avoid duplicate updates. These identifiers are not a guarantee of anonymity.

Ordinary gameplay does not require a Fraser Studios account or your name, email address or password. Local progress supports the game and stays on the device unless you enable the optional Apple features described below.

Optional iCloud Save

You can turn on iCloud Save in Settings. The app uses a dedicated private CloudKit database under your Apple account to save eligible progress: personal bests, recent Classic results, progression and statistics, completed Daily history, streaks, settings and tutorial preferences. Saves merge with local progress when the service is available; ordinary play remains available offline.

The app retains Apple account identifiers locally to detect account changes and requires reconnection before using a different account. Active attempts and rewarded retry entitlements are not restored from a cloud save. Fraser Studios does not operate a separate server holding these private saves. Apple provides the storage under its privacy policy.

Optional Game Center

If you enable Game Center in Settings, NEARLY! sends eligible personal bests, rolling 20-run Classic averages, achievement progress and new eligible Daily best scores to Apple. Scores are associated with your Game Center account. Earlier local-beta Daily scores are not uploaded.

Your Game Center nickname, avatar and scores may be visible to other players through Apple's leaderboards. Manage your profile and sharing in Apple's Game Center settings. See Game Center & Privacy. These features are optional; you can play without enabling them.

Advertising and privacy choices

NEARLY! uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads at safe gameplay breaks and optional rewarded ads for extra Daily attempts. The current TestFlight beta uses Google's TEST ad units. Test ads still require network requests and are not a promise that no technical information is processed.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions, app interactions, crash information and performance data. Processing depends on device permissions, privacy choices and Google's services. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising SDK data disclosures.

Before requesting ads, the app uses Google's consent system and checks whether ads may be requested. Settings includes “Ad privacy choices”; a form is shown when Google's system requires one. If an ad is unavailable, ordinary gameplay continues. The current app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not intentionally access the advertising identifier through a custom tracking feature. This does not imply the advertising SDK processes no identifiers or diagnostics.

Analytics, diagnostics and TestFlight

No remote gameplay analytics or separate crash-reporting provider is configured in this beta. The “Usage analytics” preference defaults to off; switching it on does not enable a remote analytics destination in this build. Advertising SDK diagnostics and Apple's beta information are separate from this preference.

Apple TestFlight may provide Fraser Studios with beta usage and crash information and feedback you choose to send, including screenshots, device and operating-system details and, depending on the invitation and feedback method, contact information. See TestFlight's privacy information. Please avoid including private information in screenshots or feedback.

Permissions and information we do not request

The current app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It has no in-app purchases, remote notifications, private messaging or custom online account database. It does not ask for your home address. Apple and Google may independently handle account and technical information when you use their services.

Children

NEARLY! is a general-audience casual game, not a service specifically directed at young children. Apple and Google services have their own age rules and parental controls. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact us below. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from young children; if you believe a child has supplied it, please contact us.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress is kept to remember your game until the app's local data is removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; offloading it may keep that data, and device backups or iCloud restoration can restore progress. There is no in-app “delete all data” feature in this version.

Turning off iCloud Save stops future app sync but does not delete an existing private cloud save. Turning off Game Center stops further app score and achievement submissions; it does not erase scores already sent to Apple. You can manage relevant Apple account and iCloud data using Apple's settings and support options. Re-enabling iCloud Save may restore previously saved progress.

Apple and Google retain information under their own policies and account controls. If you email Fraser Studios, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to respond to support or privacy requests and retain them only while needed to resolve the request, handle necessary follow-up or meet a legal obligation.

Your privacy rights

We use game information to provide the features you choose, optional service choices to enable those services, and support correspondence for our legitimate interest in answering and resolving requests. Where consent is required for advertising, choices are managed through Google's consent system. Providers explain their own processing grounds in their policies.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive personal information, restrict its use, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise rights concerning information Fraser Studios holds. Provider-managed information may require a request to Apple or Google. You may complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority.

Third-party services and this website

Apple and Google may process information outside your country, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards. This policy does not replace their policies.

This legal website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical access information when serving the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Updates

We will update this policy when the app's features or information handling changes and revise the date above. Material changes may also be explained in app or release information.
