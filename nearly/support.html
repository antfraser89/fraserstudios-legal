How to play

Every game asks you to get as close as you can: stop a timer, charge a battery, draw a line, cut a shape, fill a vessel, set an angle, find a shape's balance point or level a beam. Your score is your Precision out of 100%. Each game shows a short guide the first time; tap ? during a game to see it again.

Daily

A new Daily of five challenges opens every day at 08:00 UTC. The first attempt is free; you can watch up to five optional ads for extra attempts, six in total. Your best attempt counts. Share your result from the Daily results screen.

Mastery Path and Challenge a Friend

Mastery Path has twelve fixed levels per game. Pass a level to unlock the next and beat the higher marks for up to three stars. To challenge a friend, play a game from Challenge a Friend and share the code; your friend enters it in Challenge a Friend to play exactly the same challenge. Codes are 12 characters, and the letters O and I can be typed as 0 and 1.

Daily reminders

Turn reminders on or off in Settings > Daily reminder. If you previously declined notifications, allow them in iOS Settings > Notifications > NEARLY!.

Progress, iCloud and Game Center

Progress is saved on your device. Turn on iCloud Save in Settings to keep a private copy in your iCloud, and Game Center to compare scores and achievements. Keep every device that shares your iCloud on the latest version of NEARLY! so saves stay in step.

Ads, privacy choices and tracking

In the UK and EEA, Google's consent form appears before ads are shown; change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Apple's “Allow tracking” request appears once; change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Every game works whatever you choose.
