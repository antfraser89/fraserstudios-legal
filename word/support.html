How to play

WORD! has six modes, each with 150 levels from Easy to Expert. Every 10th level is a Special with its own twist.

  • Word Search: drag across the letters to find the hidden words. Extra words you spot score too.
  • Crossword: tap a square, read the clue and type the answer. Grids grow from minis to 9×9.
  • Word Wheel: swipe through the letters to spell every word in the grid, and fill the Bonus Bank with extras.
  • Word Guess: guess the hidden word; the colours show which letters are right. Or RISK IT on one final guess.
  • WORD BLAST!: drag letter pieces onto the board. Spell a word across or down and it blasts away.
  • Word Ladder: change one letter at a time to climb from the start word to the end word, and try to beat par.

Hints and continues

You start with 10 hints and earn more by clearing new levels, reaching new player levels and winning medals. When you run out you can watch an optional ad for three more. In some modes you can watch an optional ad to keep going when a puzzle would otherwise end.

Stars, Today goals and medals

Each level earns up to three stars. New Today goals arrive every day at midnight, your local time; finish them all for a perfect day, and perfect days in a row build your streak. Medals arrive every 25 levels you solve in a mode.

Game Center

Turn on Game Center in Settings to share your total stars on the leaderboard. If a different Game Center player is signed in on the iPhone, WORD! asks you to turn sharing off and on again before sending anything.

Ads, privacy choices and tracking

Ads show only between puzzles, never while you are solving one. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad; change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Apple's tracking request appears once; change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game works whatever you choose.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it, unless it is restored from your iPhone's own backup.
