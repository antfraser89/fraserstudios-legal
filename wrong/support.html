WRONG! Support

Contact us

Email ant.fraser1989@gmail.com with any question or problem. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. You can also use Settings > Feedback / Report a Problem in the app. Please never send your Apple ID password or payment card number.

How to play

Each question has two answers. Under the normal rule, tap the wrong one before the timer runs out. In Rule Flip and Chaos, read the rule banner every time: it can ask for the wrong answer, the right answer, the opposite, or no tap at all. Settings > How To Play shows the full guide.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-off purchase in Settings that switches off the automatic ads between runs. Optional rewarded ads for extra Daily attempts stay available. On a new iPhone, open Settings > Remove Ads > Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ad privacy choices

In the UK and EEA, Google's consent form appears before ads are shown. You can refuse and still play every mode. To change your choice later, use Settings > Ad Privacy Choices (in versions that include it).

Progress and leaderboards

Progress is saved on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, to your private iCloud so it can be restored after reinstalling. Daily, Weekly and Chaos results appear on public leaderboards under your leaderboard name or a label like PLAYER 1234. To report an abusive name or ask for an entry to be removed, email us with the name and the board.
