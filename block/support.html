How to play

BLAST! Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board and fill whole rows or columns to clear them. Clear lines again within three placements to build a streak, which multiplies the points. Press Play to choose Classic (play until no piece fits), Blitz (90 seconds, score all you can) or Trail (the weekly challenge: 100 levels with gems to collect, starting again every Monday).

OUT! Slide every block out through a gate of its colour. FIT! Fit every piece into the board; there is exactly one way. Tap ? or How to play in the game for more.

Hints and continues

A hint shows one good move. You earn a hint every three finished runs or levels, and when you run out you can watch an optional ad for three more. In BLAST! Classic you can watch an optional ad once per run to continue when no piece fits.

Daily goals, streaks and medals

Three new goals arrive every day at midnight, your local time. Finish all three for a perfect day; perfect days in a row build your streak. Stats and medals are on the Stats screen from Home.

Reminders

Turn reminders on or off in Settings > Reminders. If notifications were declined before, allow them in iOS Settings > Notifications > BLOCK!.

Game Center

Tap Connect Game Center in Settings to share your BLAST! best on the leaderboard. If a different Game Center player is signed in on the iPhone, BLOCK! asks you to connect again before sending anything.

Ads, privacy choices and tracking

Ads show only between games, never during play. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad; change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Apple's tracking request appears once; change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game works whatever you choose.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it, unless it is restored from your iPhone's own backup.
