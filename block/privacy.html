BLOCK! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This notice covers BLOCK! version 1.0 for iPhone.

BLOCK! is a set of block puzzle games (BLAST!, OUT!, FIT! and the weekly Trail) made by Fraser Studios, operated by Anthony Fraser in the United Kingdom. Anthony Fraser is the controller of personal information handled by Fraser Studios for this game. Contact details are at the end.

The short version

  • There is no BLOCK! account, and Fraser Studios runs no server for the game. Your progress stays on your iPhone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process device information to show and measure ads, as described below. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked first, and you can say no.
  • Apple asks once whether BLOCK! may use your advertising identifier. You can say no and the game works the same.
  • Game Center and reminders are off unless you turn them on.

What stays on your iPhone

BLOCK! saves your games in progress, scores and bests (Classic and Blitz), streaks, lines and other play statistics, medals, daily goals and their progress, level records and stars, weekly Trail progress and badges, hints, and your settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, high contrast, reminders). It also keeps identifiers for runs and hint grants, and, if you connect Game Center, the Game Center player identifier your best was sent to. This information is stored in the app's storage on your device so the game can resume and keep your progress. It is not uploaded to Fraser Studios. Deleting the app deletes it, although your iPhone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup, under your Apple settings) may keep a copy.

Advertising: Google AdMob

BLOCK! is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear between games, never during a run or a level: a short ad may play as you leave a results screen, at most about every four minutes of time in the app, and rewarded ads play only when you choose one (to continue a run or for three more hints). Ads are limited to general-audience content.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may receive your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system information, app and ad interaction information (such as ads shown and tapped), diagnostic information and, only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, your device's advertising identifier. Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, personalise ads. See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob's iOS data disclosure.

UK, EEA and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choices, with an option not to consent. You can change them at any time in BLOCK! under Settings > Ad privacy choices. Without consent you may still see basic, non-personalised ads, which still involve some technical processing.

Apple's tracking permission: after a short explanation in the game, Apple asks once whether BLOCK! may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads still work. Change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Game Center (optional)

If you tap Connect Game Center in Settings, Apple's Game Center signs you in and BLOCK! sends your BLAST! Classic best score to the BLAST! best leaderboard whenever it rises. Leaderboards can show your Game Center name and score to other players. Nothing else from your save is sent. You can stop sharing in Settings at any time. Game Center is Apple's service; see Apple's Game Center & Privacy.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn reminders on, BLOCK! schedules local notifications on your iPhone: an evening reminder, a reminder on an evening a daily goal streak would end, and a note when the weekly Trail resets on Monday. They are created on the device from your save; no notification server is involved and nothing is sent to Fraser Studios. Turn them off in Settings, or in iOS Settings > Notifications > BLOCK!.

What BLOCK! does not do

BLOCK! has no sign-up, chat, messaging or user-generated content, no in-app purchases in this version, and no analytics or crash-reporting service run by Fraser Studios. It does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

If you test BLOCK! through TestFlight, Apple may provide crash reports and the feedback you choose to send. See TestFlight & Privacy.

Children

BLOCK! is a general-audience game that children may play. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, it has no account or social features, and its ads are limited to general-audience content. Parents can use iOS Screen Time to manage apps, tracking and notifications. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal bases (UK and EEA)

Keeping your progress on your device is part of providing the game you asked for. Advertising personalisation, the advertising identifier and non-essential storage or access on your device are used only where you have consented through Google's form or Apple's prompt, and you can withdraw that consent at any time without losing play. Information you send us when you ask for support is used, in our legitimate interest, to answer you.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your iPhone stays until you delete the app (or its backups). Google and Apple keep information under their own policies. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request, then deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object, and to withdraw consent. Because Fraser Studios holds no BLOCK! player data on a server, most of your information is on your own device under your control; for information held by Google or Apple, use their tools linked above. You can contact us about any request, and you may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this notice before BLOCK! starts using information in a new way, for example adding a purchase, cloud saving or analytics, and change the date above.
