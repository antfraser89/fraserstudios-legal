WRONG! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

WRONG! is an iPhone game developed by Fraser Studios, operated by Anthony Fraser in the United Kingdom. Anthony Fraser is the controller of personal information handled by Fraser Studios for this game.

What you do not need to give us

Playing WRONG! does not need a Fraser Studios account, your real name, email address, phone number or password. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Information on your iPhone

The app keeps the following in its own storage on the device: personal bests and recent results (scores, mistakes, streaks and reaction times), any run in progress so you can resume it, recently shown question IDs (so the same questions do not keep repeating), your settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, relaxed timing, high contrast, reminders), and whether you have seen the how-to-play screens.

The app also creates a random player identifier and keeps your Daily and Chaos records, reward records, optional leaderboard name and any unsent feedback in a protected store on the device (the iOS Keychain), so that ranked attempts and rewards cannot be counted twice.

iCloud (CloudKit)

If you are signed in to iCloud, WRONG! saves a copy of that player record to your private iCloud database in Apple's CloudKit, so your progress can be restored on a new or reinstalled iPhone. Only your Apple ID can read your private database; Fraser Studios cannot browse it. You can remove it in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app keeps working with local data only.

Leaderboards and the public name

When you complete a ranked Daily challenge or a ranked Chaos run, the app publishes a leaderboard entry to Fraser Studios' public CloudKit database: your leaderboard name, score, number of successful answers and total valid reaction time for that period. Other players can see these entries. They do not include your Apple ID, email address or iCloud name.

The leaderboard name is optional. If you do not set one, the board shows a label such as PLAYER 1234 derived from your random player identifier. If you set one, it must be 3 to 20 letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens or underscores. The rules are not a profanity filter: if a name is abusive, email us and we will consider removing it.

Game Center

The app can connect to Apple's Game Center if you are signed in to it on your iPhone. When Game Center leaderboards are enabled for WRONG!, eligible Daily and Chaos scores may be submitted to Apple and shown with your Game Center alias. Manage your Game Center profile and visibility in iOS Settings > Game Center. See Game Center & Privacy.

Invite a friend

Each player has a short invite code. If you enter a friend's code before your first Daily, the app records the link between the two codes (and later whether you completed a Daily) in the public CloudKit database, so the inviter can receive a bonus. The record contains the codes only, not names or contact details. Sending an invite uses the iPhone share sheet: you choose the app and the recipient, and Fraser Studios does not receive their details.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, the app stores your chosen category, the text you type (up to 2,000 characters) and basic context (app version, platform, screen and game mode) in Fraser Studios' CloudKit container so we can read it. Do not include personal information you do not want us to have. Feedback is not shown to other players.

Sharing scores

The Share buttons open the iPhone share sheet with a short score message. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Reminders

If you turn on Retry Reminders, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules one local reminder on your iPhone when your next Daily retry unlocks. This is not a push notification service: nothing is sent to Fraser Studios or another company.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

WRONG! is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad at a natural break after about four minutes of active play, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for an extra ranked Daily attempt. Ads are never shown in the middle of a question.

WRONG! requests non-personalised ads only. It does not use your information to build an advertising profile or to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and the app itself does not ask for Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalised ads still involve some technical information: Google's SDK can process your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance data, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Google's own notices are the source for its processing: AdMob iOS data disclosure and the Google privacy policy.

Before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form where it is required (for example in the UK and EEA). If you refuse, or ads cannot be shown, you can still play every mode; you may just not be able to watch a rewarded ad for an extra Daily attempt. You can review your choices from the consent message when Google offers it again, and you can turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Remove Ads (planned)

A one-off Remove Ads purchase is planned but is not in the current version. If it is added, Apple will handle the payment, Fraser Studios will not receive card details, and this notice will be updated first.

Services WRONG! does not use

No Fraser Studios account system, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK (such as Firebase, Crashlytics or Sentry), no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and no sale of player information. The iPhone app does not use the older Supabase Daily service; that service is retained only as a developer fallback for non-iPhone builds.

If you test a TestFlight beta, Apple may give Fraser Studios usage and crash information and any feedback you choose to send. See TestFlight privacy information.

Children

WRONG! is a general-audience game and is not designed for young children, though younger players may enjoy it. It does not ask for contact details, does not require a public name, and requests non-personalised ads only. A parent or guardian can email us about a child's information; giving the leaderboard name or invite code helps us find it.

How long information is kept

Device data stays until you delete the app (iPhone backups may restore it). Your private iCloud copy stays until you delete it in iCloud settings. Public leaderboard, invite and feedback records are kept while the game runs so boards and rewards stay correct; email us to ask for removal and include your leaderboard name or invite code so we can find them. Support emails are kept while needed to resolve the request. Google keeps advertising data under its own policy.

Who receives information and international transfers

Apple hosts iCloud/CloudKit and Game Center data and processes App Store downloads and any future purchase (Apple privacy policy). Google processes advertising information. Both may process data outside the UK under their own safeguards. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages without tracking scripts or cookies; GitHub may process technical access information to serve the site (GitHub privacy statement).

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your information, ask us to restrict or stop using it, ask for a portable copy, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier lawful processing). Email ant.fraser1989@gmail.com. We aim to respond within one month and may ask for details that help us find your records, never your password.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF, 0303 123 1113. In the EEA you can also complain to your local authority.

Changes

We will update this notice and its date before the app's handling of information changes, for example when Remove Ads, Game Center leaderboards or personalised advertising options are added.
