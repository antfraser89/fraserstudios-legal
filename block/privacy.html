BLOCK! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

BLOCK! is an iPhone puzzle game developed by Fraser Studios, operated by Anthony Fraser in the United Kingdom. Anthony Fraser is the controller of personal information handled by Fraser Studios for this game. Contact details are below.

This notice describes the current pre-release Unity version. Advertising and consent-dependent tracking are disabled. No BLOCK! TestFlight build has yet been made available to install. We will review this notice and the app's privacy controls before enabling advertising or adding another data service.

Progress and preferences on your device

BLOCK! saves your current games, scores, personal bests, moves and play times, level unlocks, stars, XP, achievements, hints, Daily results and streaks, tutorial choices, and sound, haptic and accessibility settings. It also stores run and reward identifiers, timestamps, completed-ad records, privacy choices and their version, verified purchase records, and eligible Game Center submission queues. These support exact resume, rewards, account separation and recovery; identifiers are not a guarantee of anonymity.

Ordinary play does not require a Fraser Studios account, name, email address or password. The game does not upload your full local save to a Fraser Studios server. Local backups and original save-format archives may be retained in the app's storage to recover progress safely.

Apple purchases and Unity In-App Purchasing

BLOCK! offers a non-consumable Remove Forced Ads purchase. It removes forced interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available only when advertising is enabled and you choose to use them. Apple handles checkout and payment details. Fraser Studios does not receive your card number.

On supported iPhone builds, the app connects to the App Store to load its price and refresh ownership, including at startup. Purchase and restore operations use Unity In-App Purchasing and Apple's StoreKit. The app verifies Apple transaction information and stores transaction and original-transaction identifiers, the product and store, a verification time and proof hash, and ownership or revocation status locally. These records prevent duplicate grants and support restoration and refunds.

Apple and Unity may process transaction and technical information to operate these services. Unity's IAP privacy documentation describes installation, player or session identifiers, device information and country, with additional identifiers depending on enabled features. BLOCK! does not supply a custom email or analytics user identifier to this integration, and does not use a webshop or third-party checkout. See App Store & Privacy, Unity IAP privacy information and Unity's player privacy policy.

Optional Game Center

You can choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center from the game. Eligible BLAST and DROP best scores and Daily scores, completion time and star information can then be submitted to Apple and associated with your Game Center account. BLOCK! keeps account identifiers and pending score records locally to separate accounts and retry eligible submissions. Signing in does not upload all historical local progress.

Leaderboards can display your Game Center alias, scores, ranks and Daily results to other players. The game also retrieves other players' leaderboard information from Apple. Manage your Game Center account and profile visibility in Apple's settings. Ordinary play remains available without Game Center. There is no in-app Game Center account deletion control; contact Apple for account or previously submitted score requests. See Game Center & Privacy.

Cloud services

The current BLOCK! build does not enable iCloud/CloudKit save synchronisation, Unity Cloud Save, or a Fraser Studios gameplay account database. Your Apple device's own backup and restore services may nevertheless preserve app data under your Apple settings. If optional cloud saving is added, we will explain its contents, provider, controls and retention before asking you to enable it; this notice does not claim that feature is already available.

Advertising, mediation and identifiers

The app includes Unity LevelPlay mediation and ironSource advertising support for interstitials at natural gameplay breaks and optional rewarded ads. The current app configuration keeps the advertising SDK uninitialised until an approved policy, privacy explanation and an eligible user choice are in place. Registering provider resources does not mean ads or tracking are running.

When advertising is enabled, Unity/ironSource and participating advertising partners may receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred from them, device and app identifiers, operating-system information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted and chosen, personalisation. Basic or non-personalised ads can still involve technical information and storage or access on your device; they are not an assurance of anonymous or consent-free processing. See Unity's player privacy policy.

Google AdMob is not currently integrated or configured as a BLOCK! mediation network. Another game's Google identifiers are not used by BLOCK!. If AdMob is added, this notice and the consent implementation will be updated before it receives player information. Google's SDK documentation describes potential processing of IP address, device identifiers, ad and app interactions and diagnostics; see AdMob's iOS data disclosures and Google's privacy policy.

The current build does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Apple's permission is separate from consent needed for advertising or storage/access technologies under UK or European law. We will not enable consent-dependent advertising or cross-app tracking merely because this policy is published. You can use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to control Apple's tracking permission.

Your advertising choices

No advertising choice is required to play the current build, because ads are disabled. The prepared Ad Privacy screen contains basic and personalised options, but a complete refusal and withdrawal control is not yet available. These controls and any required consent-management integration must be completed and reviewed before advertising is enabled. We will explain the recipients and purposes and provide a way to refuse or withdraw consent without losing ordinary gameplay. Removing forced ads is not a purchase of consent and does not automatically authorise optional rewarded ads or tracking.

Analytics, diagnostics and beta feedback

No separate remote gameplay analytics, Firebase or crash-reporting service is configured by Fraser Studios in BLOCK!. This does not mean all Unity, Apple or advertising SDK processing is absent. SDK service diagnostics and purchasing data are separate from a dedicated gameplay analytics service; their applicable notices are linked above. We have not validated native network traffic yet and will review SDK disclosures before distribution.

When a TestFlight build becomes available, Apple may provide beta usage and crash information and feedback you choose to send, including screenshots, device or operating-system details and, depending on how feedback is sent, contact information. Do not include private information in screenshots or feedback. See TestFlight privacy information.

Support and information we do not request

If you contact Fraser Studios, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to answer your support or privacy request. The game does not ask for your home address or access to precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library. It has no chat or private messaging feature. Apple and enabled service providers handle their own account information under their policies.

Purposes and UK/EEA legal bases

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, information necessary to deliver the game, a purchase or an optional service you request is used to perform that service or contract. Our legitimate interests in providing support, maintaining reliable services and preventing abuse may support necessary support and security processing; these interests must be balanced against your rights. Information required for accounting, regulatory responses or other legal duties is processed to meet those duties.

We will seek consent for advertising personalisation, tracking and non-essential storage or access where required, before enabling that processing. UK PECR and corresponding European rules can apply to mobile apps, not only website cookies. Choosing contextual ads or granting Apple's tracking permission does not by itself settle these requirements. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Apple, Unity and any future advertising partners explain their own roles and bases in their notices.

Retention and deletion

Local game and recovery records are retained to remember and recover progress until app data is removed. There is no in-app delete-all-data feature in this build. Deleting the app normally removes its local files, while offloading, device backups or Apple restoration may preserve or restore them. Deleting local purchase records does not cancel a purchase; restoring ownership may recreate those records.

Removing BLOCK! or signing out of Game Center does not automatically erase information already held by Apple or another provider. Their retention and deletion procedures are described in their notices. For Unity IAP data processed on our behalf, contact Fraser Studios so we can handle and route your request; where Unity is an independent controller, you may also need to contact Unity. We do not promise deletion of records that a provider must keep for fraud prevention, accounting or legal duties.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve your request, handle necessary follow-up or legal claims, and meet applicable legal obligations. We use the nature of the request and those requirements to determine retention; we do not retain it indefinitely for unrelated marketing.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the applicable law and processing basis, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal information and object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact Fraser Studios using the details below; we may ask for proportionate information to verify and locate your records, not your Apple password or payment card details. Some rights have legal exceptions.

We do not operate an advertising profile database or make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects using an automated decision system. Advertising providers may use information to select or measure ads under their own policies. You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office or the competent supervisory authority in the EEA country where you live, work or believe a breach occurred.

Children

BLOCK! is a general-audience puzzle game, not specifically directed at young children. We do not knowingly ask young children to provide personal contact details. Apple and other providers have their own age and parental controls. A parent or guardian concerned about a child's information can contact Fraser Studios. The app's general-audience classification does not remove applicable children's privacy protections.

Recipients, international processing and this website

Relevant recipients are Apple for purchases, Game Center, backups and TestFlight; Unity/ironSource for the services described above when enabled; and the service hosting support correspondence. They may process information outside the UK or EEA. Applicable safeguards can include adequacy decisions or approved contractual safeguards, as described in their notices. Contact us for information about a particular transfer concerning information we control. We will assess required safeguards before adding a new provider.

This website uses GitHub Pages. We add no tracking scripts, cookies, analytics or external fonts. GitHub may process technical access information to serve and secure it; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this notice when features, providers or information handling change. The date and relevant release information will identify the revision. Material new consent-dependent purposes will require an appropriate new choice, not silent reuse of an old preference.
